A useful starting point

Can your business run clearly when one key person is unavailable?

If important work lives in someone's head, updates are scattered across chats or handoffs depend on guesswork, your business may be carrying avoidable operational risk.

  • Unclear ownership or repeated follow-ups
  • Processes that vary depending on who does the work
  • Projects with limited visibility of risks or decisions
  • Tools that are in place but not used consistently
  • Knowledge that is difficult to transfer or maintain
